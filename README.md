# FUTURE_DS_01
A real-world sales analytics project focused on transforming, analyzing, and visualizing business data to generate actionable insights on revenue trends, product performance, and profitability using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, with Power Query for data cleaning and transformation and DAX for KPI development.
📊 Business Sales Performance Analysis
🧩 Project Overview

This is a real-world sales analytics project focused on transforming, analyzing, and visualizing business data to generate actionable insights on revenue trends, product performance, profitability, and regional growth opportunities.

The goal is to simulate the work of a real data analyst by helping a business understand where to focus efforts to improve revenue and profitability.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel – initial data exploration and validation
Power BI – dashboard development and data visualization
Power Query (Power BI) – data cleaning, transformation, and ETL processes
DAX – KPI creation and calculated business measures
🎯 Project Objectives
Identify top revenue-generating products
Analyse sales and profit trends over time
Evaluate product profitability and performance
Compare regional and city-level performance
Provide data-driven business recommendations
🔄 Data Processing

The dataset was cleaned and prepared using Power Query in Power BI, including:

Removing duplicates and null values
Standardizing data formats
Creating structured tables for analysis
Preparing data for modelling and visualization

Key business metrics and KPIs were created using DAX, including:

Total Sales
Total Profit
Profit Margin
Customer Count
Average Order Value (AOV)
📊 Key Insights
💰 Product Performance
The highest revenue-generating product is Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier
Some high-revenue products (e.g., Cisco TelePresence System) are unprofitable
Certain low-volume products show extreme losses and require review
📈 Sales Trends (2014–2017)
Sales increased by 51.4%
Profit increased by 88.6%
Growth was driven by both increased order volume and improving profitability
Average Order Value showed a declining trend, but was offset by higher order frequency
🌍 Regional & City Performance
West Region is the most profitable region
Central Region is the weakest performing region
New York City is the highest profit-generating city (62.04K)
Philadelphia is a loss-making city requiring intervention
💡 Strategic Recommendations
Invest more in high-performing regions (especially West Region)
Review or discontinue consistently unprofitable products
Strengthen marketing in top-performing cities (New York, Los Angeles)
Investigate and improve Central Region performance
Focus on customer retention and repeat purchase strategies
📌 Key Outcome

This project demonstrates end-to-end data analytics skills including data cleaning, transformation, modelling, visualization, and business insight generation. It highlights how data can be used to support strategic decision-making in a real business environment.
